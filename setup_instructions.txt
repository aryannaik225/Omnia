===============================================================================
                    OMNIA - SETUP GUIDE (for everyone)
       All your streaming services in one app, on your PC, phone and TV
===============================================================================

This guide walks you through three things:

  PART 1.  Installing Omnia on your Windows PC
  PART 2.  Finding the PIN and QR code in Omnia's settings
  PART 3.  Opening Omnia on your phone or TV using a web browser
  PART 4.  (Optional) Syncing your library with Omnia on the web

No technical knowledge is needed. It takes about 5 minutes.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT YOU NEED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC, connected to the internet.
  * The Omnia installer file:  Omnia_<version>_x64-setup.exe
    (for example Omnia_1.2.0_x64-setup.exe, from the GitHub Releases page)
  * For phones / TVs: the phone or TV must be on the SAME Wi-Fi as the PC.


===============================================================================
 PART 1 - INSTALL OMNIA ON YOUR PC
===============================================================================

 1. Find the file "Omnia_<version>_x64-setup.exe" (usually in your Downloads
    folder) and double-click it.

 2. Windows might show a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC".
    This appears for new apps that Windows doesn't know yet.
      -> Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".

 3. The Omnia installer opens. Click "Next" / "Install" and wait until it
    says it has finished. You do NOT need an administrator password; Omnia
    installs just for your Windows user.

    (On some Windows 10 PCs the installer quietly downloads a small Microsoft
    component called "WebView2" first. That's normal and needs internet.)

 4. Click "Finish". Omnia opens automatically. You'll also find it in the
    Start menu: press the Windows key and type "Omnia".

 5. FIRST LAUNCH
      * Omnia shows a "Who's watching?" screen. Click your profile or create
        one (you can add one for each family member, and kids profiles too).
      * Omnia works out your country by itself. To change it, click the globe
        button at the top right (for example "IN" or "US").
      * In the first minutes Omnia quietly downloads its recommendation
        engine (about 25 MB). You can keep using the app meanwhile.

 6. RECOMMENDED: tell Omnia which services you pay for
      * Open Settings (the gear icon at the bottom of the left-hand menu).
      * Under "My Subscriptions", tick the services you use (Netflix, Prime
        Video, JioHotstar, ...).
      * Then click "My Subscriptions" in the top bar to only see titles you
        can watch without paying extra.

 Good to know:
   * Closing the Omnia window keeps it running in the background, as a small
     icon near the clock at the bottom right of your screen (the "tray"). To
     fully quit, right-click that icon and choose Quit.
   * Everything Omnia knows about you stays on THIS PC. Nothing is uploaded
     unless you choose to sign in to sync (see PART 4).
   * Omnia updates itself: when a new version is ready it asks you to
     "Restart to Install".


===============================================================================
 PART 2 - FIND THE PIN AND QR CODE (to connect a phone or TV)
===============================================================================

 Phones and TVs connect to Omnia ON YOUR PC over your home Wi-Fi. First you
 switch this on and get a pairing PIN:

 1. In Omnia on your PC, open Settings (gear icon, bottom of the left menu).

 2. Scroll to the section called  "Linked Devices".

 3. Turn on the switch:  "Allow phones & TVs on this network".

 4. If Windows asks whether to allow Omnia on networks, choose
    "Private networks" (your home Wi-Fi) and click "Allow".
    Do not choose public networks.

 5. The Linked Devices section now shows:
      * a 6-DIGIT PIN             (for example  482 913)
      * a QR CODE                 (a black-and-white square to scan)
      * your PC's ADDRESS         (looks like  http://192.168.1.23:8765 )
      * a list of paired devices, each with a "Revoke" button

 About the PIN:
   * It changes every 10 minutes and after every successful pairing, so
     always use the one currently on screen.
   * Need a fresh one? Click "New PIN".
   * You only pair each phone or TV ONCE. After that it is remembered.
   * Lost a phone or want to disconnect a device? Click "Revoke" next to it.


===============================================================================
 PART 3 - OPEN OMNIA ON YOUR PHONE OR TV (web browser, port 8765)
===============================================================================

 Before you start:
   * Your PC must be switched on, with Omnia running (the tray icon is fine).
   * The phone / TV must be on the SAME Wi-Fi as the PC (not a "guest" Wi-Fi).
   * Keep Settings > Linked Devices open on the PC so you can see the PIN.

 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  OPTION A - PHONE, EASIEST: SCAN THE QR CODE
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open your phone's Camera app.
  2. Point it at the QR code on your PC screen.
  3. Tap the link that pops up. Omnia opens in your phone's browser and
     pairs automatically. Done!

 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  OPTION B - PHONE, TABLET OR TV: TYPE THE ADDRESS
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open the web browser (Safari on iPhone/iPad, Chrome on Android, or the
     "Internet" / "Browser" app on a smart TV).

  2. In the ADDRESS BAR at the top (not a Google search box), type your PC's
     address EXACTLY as shown in Linked Devices, including ":8765" at the end.
     For example:

            http://192.168.1.23:8765

     * Start with  http://  (NOT https://)
     * The numbers are different for every home; copy yours from the PC.
     * 8765 is Omnia's "port". It must be there, after a colon.

  3. A pairing page appears. Type the 6-digit PIN shown on your PC and
     confirm.

  4. That's it: you're browsing your Omnia library. Pick your profile on
     the "Who's watching?" screen.

 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TIPS
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Make it an app icon:
      - iPhone (Safari):  tap Share -> "Add to Home Screen".
      - Android (Chrome): tap the three-dot menu -> "Add to Home screen".
    Next time just tap the icon; no PIN needed.

  * Pressing PLAY on a phone opens the title in the real Netflix / Prime
    Video / JioHotstar app on that phone (when it's installed). What you
    watch there syncs back to Omnia on the PC automatically.

  * Use your phone as a REMOTE: open the "Remote" tab on the phone to control
    Omnia on your PC (or on a TV running Omnia): arrows, OK, Back, Play/Pause.

  * Each phone or TV keeps its own chosen profile, so the kids' tablet can
    stay on the kids profile.


===============================================================================
 PART 4 - (OPTIONAL) SYNC YOUR LIBRARY WITH OMNIA ON THE WEB
===============================================================================

 Omnia also runs in any web browser (the "web app" at /app on Omnia's
 website), as a simple tracker: mark movies and episodes as watched, keep a
 watchlist, see what's coming up. It works as a guest with no account.

 To keep the PC and the web app in step:

 1. In Omnia on your PC, open Settings and find "Sign in to Sync".
 2. Sign in with Google, or with an email and password.
 3. Sign in with the SAME account in the web app.

 Your watchlist, watched movies and episodes, Home page layout, profile name
 and picture then show up on both. Only the Omnia profile you signed in with
 syncs. Your streaming-service logins, detailed viewing data and
 recommendation data never leave your PC. Sign out at any time to stop
 syncing; your local data stays.


===============================================================================
 TROUBLESHOOTING
===============================================================================

 "This site can't be reached" / the page never loads on the phone or TV
   -> Is the PC on, with Omnia running (check the tray icon near the clock)?
   -> Is the phone / TV on the SAME Wi-Fi as the PC? Not a guest network,
      and not mobile data (turn Wi-Fi on, mobile data doesn't reach your PC).
   -> Is "Allow phones & TVs on this network" switched ON in Linked Devices?
   -> Did you type  http://  (not https://)  and  :8765  at the end?
   -> Windows firewall may have blocked it. Open Windows Settings ->
      "Privacy & security" -> "Windows Security" -> "Firewall & network
      protection" -> "Allow an app through firewall", find Omnia and tick
      "Private". Then try again.
   -> Some routers block devices from talking to each other ("AP isolation"
      or "client isolation"). If nothing else works, check your router's
      Wi-Fi settings or try a different Wi-Fi band (2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz).

 "Wrong PIN" or "PIN expired"
   -> The PIN changes every 10 minutes and after each pairing. Use the one
      currently on the PC screen, or click "New PIN".
   -> After 5 wrong tries the PIN is replaced for safety; use the new one.

 The PC's address changed (the phone stopped connecting after a few days)
   -> Your router may give the PC a new address now and then. Check the
      address in Linked Devices again and update your phone's bookmark or
      home-screen icon.

 Windows says "Windows protected your PC" during install
   -> Click "More info" -> "Run anyway". (Shown for apps that are new to
      Windows.)

 Omnia on the PC says "Connect TMDB to start browsing"
   -> Open Settings and paste the TMDB key you were given, or ask the person
      who gave you Omnia.


===============================================================================
 PRIVACY & SAFETY, IN SHORT
===============================================================================

  * Your watch history, profiles and recommendations are stored only on your
    PC. No account is needed. Cloud sync is optional and off until you sign
    in (PART 4), and even then your streaming logins never leave the PC.
  * Phones and TVs read everything directly from your PC over your own
    Wi-Fi. Only devices you paired with the PIN can get in, and you can
    revoke any of them at any time.
  * The phone/TV connection is meant for your HOME Wi-Fi. Don't switch it on
    while your PC is on public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports, hotels).
  * Paired phones and TVs cannot delete profiles, wipe history or change
    Omnia's settings on your PC.
